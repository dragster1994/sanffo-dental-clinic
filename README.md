## ✨ Features & Architecture

1. **Brand Identity & Refined Medical Theme**
   * **Headers, Primary Texts, High Authority Accents**: Deep Navy (`#0B2545` / `#071930`) providing authoritative clinical confidence.
   * **Primary Buttons, Highlighted Services, Active Tabs**: Vibrant Teal Cyan (`#0891B2` / `#06B6D4`) providing eye-catching interactive conversion focal points.
   * **Backgrounds, Hero Sections, Cards Container**: Refreshing Soft Mint Ice (`#F0FAF7` / `#E6F6F1` / `#E8F7F3`) delivering a soothing, sterile, modern dental atmosphere.
   * **Body Paragraphs & Subtext**: Crisp Slate Grey (`#334155` / `#475569` / `#64748B`) ensuring readable typography with zero eye fatigue.

2. **Complete Section Flow (Per Exact Specifications)**
   * **Sticky Header & Blur Navigation**: Responsive desktop menu, mobile drawer, quick "Book Appointment" CTA.
   * **Hero Section**: Value proposition, prominent callouts, doctor credential badge, floating trust badge, and instant booking CTAs.
   * **Quick Trust Bar**: 4-point credential summary with modern line icons.
   * **About the Clinic**: Comprehensive overview of SANFFO's patient-centered clinical care and treatments.
   * **Why Choose Us**: 6 core clinical differentiators.
   * **Services & Treatments**: Interactive filtering (General, Restorative, Endodontics, Cosmetic, Surgery, Prosthetics, Pediatric, Emergency) with "Learn More" detail modal dialogs.
   * **Featured Treatment ("Save Your Natural Smile")**: Dedicated spotlight on conservative Root Canal therapy.
   * **Meet Your Dentist**: Profile of Dr. Syed Fahad Ali Shah with official credentials (BDS, RDS, MPhil, CHPE).
   * **Patient Journey**: 4-step clear treatment pathway (Book &rarr; Assessment &rarr; Plan &rarr; Care).
   * **Appointment Booking Form**: Full interactive form with field validation, required clinical indicators, treatment selector (13 specialties), and direct one-click WhatsApp prefilled message integration.
   * **WhatsApp Direct CTA**: Pre-filled template: `"Hello SANFFO DENTAL CLINIC, I would like to book a dental appointment."`
   * **Emergency Dental Section**: Clear triage advice, click-to-call, and WhatsApp urgent chat.
   * **Smile Transformations (Before & After)**: Interactive draggable comparison slider across cosmetic, restorative, and whitening treatments.
   * **Clinic Gallery**: Categorized image grid with interactive fullscreen Lightbox viewer (keyboard arrow & Esc support).
   * **Patient Testimonials**: Realistic patient review cards with Google Reviews link.
   * **Accordion FAQ**: 14 essential questions answered, including compliant treatment cost guidance.
   * **Location & Map**: Landmark location description, interactive Google Maps embed, and turn-by-turn directions trigger.
   * **Contact Section**: Contact info cards for Phone, WhatsApp, Manager Waqas Khan, and quick direct message form.
   * **Multi-column Footer**: Quick links, clinical services, contact info, copyright, and accessible Privacy Policy & Terms modals.
   * **Floating Action Buttons**: Desktop floating pill/circle buttons and mobile bottom action bar for Call, WhatsApp, and Online Booking.

3. **SEO & Accessibility (Local SEO Optimized)**
   * Semantic HTML5 (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<aside>`, `<footer>`).
   * Schema.org `Dentist` JSON-LD structured data for Google Local Search.
   * OpenGraph tags for social media link sharing.
   * WCAG 2.1 AA accessible color contrast, keyboard navigable controls, and focus states.

## 🚀 Running Locally

To preview the website locally:

### Option 1: Double click
Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Safari).

### Option 2: Local HTTP Server (PowerShell / Python)
In this directory:
```powershell
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit: [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)
